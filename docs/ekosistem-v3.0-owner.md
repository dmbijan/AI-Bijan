# AI BIJAN SaaS Ecosystem V4.5 (18/10/2026)

Platform perniagaan SaaS berasaskan Laravel — Your AI Business Team, in the Room.

## Struktur 7 Kumpulan & 18 B-Modules

### 1. Operasi Dalaman & Pasukan
- **B-Virtual Office** — Ruang kerja syarikat, pasukan AI, tugasan, pengumuman
- **B-HRMS** — Pekerja, kehadiran, cuti, payroll Malaysia
- **B-Sign & Docs** — Dokumen, templat, tandatangan elektronik
- **B-Projects** — Projek, Kanban, milestone, Gantt, time tracking
- **B-Assets** — Daftar aset, susut nilai, penyelenggaraan

### 2. Jualan, Saluran & Pertumbuhan
- **B-POS System** — POS retail/F&B, QR Order, pelbagai pembayaran
- **B-Site** — Pembina laman web, landing page, sales funnel
- **B-Link** — Micro-site pautan bio, katalog, QR codes
- **B-Marketplace** — Storefront, troli, checkout, pesanan
- **B-Affiliate** — Agen, dropship, komisen automatik

### 3. Pelanggan, Pemasaran & Sokongan
- **B-Loyalty** — Mata ganjaran, tier, baucar, promosi
- **B-Omnichannel** — Peti masuk berpusat, WhatsApp, Instagram, e-mel
- **B-Booking** — Perkhidmatan, slot, jadual, deposit
- **B-Marketing** — Segmen, kempen, automasi, Ads Manager (Google/Meta)
- **B-Helpdesk / B-Desk** — Tiket, SLA, knowledge base

### 4. Stok, Pengeluaran & COGS
- **B-COGS Engine** — Kos jualan automatik, margin per SKU
- **B-Inventory & Warehouse** — Stok real-time, multi-lokasi, batch/luput
- **B-BOM & Manufacturing** — Resipi/BOM, work order, kos pengeluaran

### 5. Kewangan, Pembelian & Logistik
- **B-Finance** — Carta akaun, jurnal, P&L, aliran tunai, kunci kira-kira
- **B-Invoicing** — Invois, sebut harga, credit note, e-Invois LHDN
- **B-Procurement** — PO, RFQ, goods received, supplier bill
- **B-Logistics** — Penghantaran, kurier, tracking, proof of delivery

### 6. Kebolehcapaian & Infrastruktur
- **B-Connect / API Gateway** — Connector registry, webhook, adapter
- **B-Auth & Security** — SSO, 2FA, RBAC, pengasingan tenant

### 7. Dashboard & Intelligence
- **B-Analytics** — KPI dashboard, laporan operasi, export
- **B-Brain Hub** — AI agents, automasi, analisis, ramalan

## Multi-Tier Admin Hierarchy (v4.5)
- **Master Admin** → Bijan Holdings (platform owner)
- **Super Admin** → Platform operator
- **White-label Admin** → Reseller/agency dengan branding sendiri
- **Customer Admin** → Tenant pelanggan akhir

## AI Token Wallet (v4.5)
Sistem kredit AI berasingan daripada langganan SaaS. Pelanggan boleh beli token tambahan bila-bila masa.

## Industry Blueprint Engine (v4.5)
Onboarding automatik mengikut jenis perniagaan Malaysia. Pilih industri → terima konfigurasi modul, roles, workflow dan dashboard yang relevan.
