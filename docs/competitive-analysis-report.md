## Executive Summary

Laporan ini menganalisis kedudukan sistem AI BIJAN berbanding 10 platform utama di pasaran global dan tempatan. Analisis merangkumi:

1. **Feature Parity Matrix** — Perbandingan ciri-ciri dengan setiap pesaing
2. **Market Positioning Map** — Di mana AI BIJAN berdiri dalam landscape
3. **Gap Analysis** — Kekurangan kritikal yang perlu diisi
4. **Differentiation Strategy** — Unfair advantages yang boleh dibina
5. **Recommended New Modules** — Untuk melangkaui semua pesaing

### Key Findings

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **AI BIJAN covers ~60%** of features across all competitors combined | No single system has what AI BIJAN plans to offer holistically |
| **Critical gaps in 4 areas** | Supply Chain, Advanced HR/Payroll, Enterprise Security, Hardware Integration |
| **Unfair advantage exists** | AI-native + Malaysia-first + Collective Intelligence = unique positioning |
| **Missing: ERP-grade depth** | Finance, Manufacturing, and Supply Chain need enterprise-level sophistication |
| **Opportunity window** | Global South markets are underserved by Western ERP systems |
